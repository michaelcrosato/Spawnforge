# Milestone 9.6: the new themes across 20 seeds

`node scripts/theme-sheet.ts <theme> --out <theme>.png` at the 9.6 commit: seeds 1 to 20 of each
new theme, generated with no constraints and drawn from three-quarters at low quality.

| Theme | Plans drawn (of 20) | Reads as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [dragon](dragon.png) | quadruped 13, wyvern 7 | Winged dragons and wyverns with folded leathery wings, horns, crests and tail spikes; scales in greens, blues, purples, black and gold |
| [aquatic](aquatic.png) | fish 13, quadruped 4, serpent 3 | Sharks and fish with dorsal and tail fins, finned eels, crocodile-like swimmers with back scutes; countershaded blues and teals, some spotted with glow |
| [eldritch](eldritch.png) | serpent 9, octopod 6, quadruped 5 | Krakens with two or three pairs of arms, spiders with clusters of eyes, two- and three-headed hounds with tentacle faces; purples with veins, slime and glow |
| [beast](beast.png) | quadruped 20 | Muscled boars, bulls, bears and cats on paws or cloven hooves, with ears, fangs, and now and then horns, tusks or bristles; striped, spotted and rosetted coats |

While tuning, bipeds and centaurs were tried in `eldritch` and `beast` and read as columns at
this size, so the themes leave them out; `beast`'s torsos were slimmed and its legs lengthened.
