# Motion review

Each filmstrip (m01.png … m10.png) shows one of the tasks below: frames left to right,
then top to bottom, with the time on each frame and a footfall diagram or an event timeline
beneath. Names of creatures, gaits, actions and events are hidden. Match every filmstrip
to the task it shows; each task is used once. Write `motion-answers.json` beside this file:
`{ "m01": "<task id>", … }`.

| Task id | Task |
| --- | --- |
| m01-horse-gallop | A horse galloping across flat ground at about 12 m/s. |
| m02-raptor-run | A two-legged raptor running at about 8 m/s. |
| m03-cat-pounce | A big cat pouncing on a target 4 m ahead of it. |
| m04-shark-turn | A shark cruising through open water, then turning to swim off to one side. |
| m05-crocodile-lake | A crocodile walking into a lake, swimming across it and climbing out on the far side. |
| m06-dragon-flight | A dragon taking off, circling, gliding and landing on a slope. |
| m07-moth-hover | A moth taking off and hovering in place. |
| m08-hydra-bite-left | A many-headed hydra biting a target on its left side with its nearest head. |
| m09-bear-hit-die | A bear that is hit hard from its left, staggers, and later dies. |
| m10-turtle-dive | A sea turtle diving to 3 m below the surface and coming back up to it. |
