# Gate 8 blind review notes

Reviewer: blind pass over `blind/r01.png` to `blind/r20.png`, matched one-to-one to the 20 prompts. All 20 were matched without a conflict. Confidence is per image; the points under "wrong or missing" are measured against the prompt text only.

**r01 -> p08-boar (confidence: high).** A stocky brown beast, 2.0 m long, with short legs, a very large head, two white tusks curving up from the lower jaw, a ridge of dark bristles down the spine and a tiny stub of a tail (rear view). The only rival is the cave bear, but this one has tusks and small paws, no big claws. Wrong or missing: the legs are so short and stubby that the belly nearly drags and the feet are tiny dark nubs, so it reads as a hairy sack more than a boar. The head does not separate from the body (no neck, no broad snout disc or rooting nose). One tusk in the head and 3/4 views points straight forward like a spike and looks like a nose horn rather than a tusk. The mouth is a thin grin of tiny teeth. The ears are small cones. The fur is a uniform brown fuzz with soft dark mottling and nothing reads as bristly boar hide.

**r02 -> p05-swamp-troll (confidence: high).** Upright, 2.0 m tall, long arms that hang to below the knees, a wide flat dome of a head and mottled green skin (a dark and light green camouflage blotch, the best match to "mottled" in the set). Wrong or missing: the "tusks" are pale horns on the brow and cheek (the top view shows them rising from the top of the head), not tusks rising from the lower jaw past the mouth. The head is held tilted back so it stares at the sky in the 3/4 and side views. The torso is a smooth tube with no neck and no waist; the legs are short, bent and close together, ending in small feet with pale toenail dots. The hands are thin and claw-like and the arms taper to nothing. The eyes are tiny and the mouth a wide grin of small teeth. The pose is stiff and a bit hunched, with little troll bulk in the shoulders.

**r03 -> p06-ram-demon (confidence: high).** A biped, 2.1 m tall, red with black horizontal stripes, and big tan horns that sweep back and curl; two fangs show at the mouth. Wrong or missing: in the top view the two horns meet in the middle of the forehead and fuse into one band, so they read as a single crescent, and they look glued on top of the head rather than growing from the sides at the temples (a ram's horns curl around the ear). The body is a fat tube with no neck, the arms are short and tucked in with black claws, and thin dark outline artifacts run down the arm and torso seams. The legs are long and thin with tiny feet and a bent, crouched stance, and there is no tail. The stripes are even, regular bands, more tiger than demon, and the stripe edges look slightly smeared at the rear. The eyes are small yellow slits.

**r04 -> p01-wolf (confidence: medium-high).** Grey fur, a paler belly and a long snout with pointed ears and long thin legs. Wrong or missing: the tail is not bushy. The rear 3/4 and top views show a thick tapered cone that continues the body line, so the back half looks like an otter or fish rather than a wolf with a brush tail. The body is long, low and narrow (29 cm wide), the chest is thin and the head reads more crocodile or fox than wolf (flat tapering muzzle with no stop). The fur is a speckled halo of single strands that looks noisy and pixelated at the silhouette and in the front view, and the pale belly is a patchy light smear rather than a clean marking. The eyes are tiny (one pale dot) and the mouth and teeth are barely visible. The ears are fine.

**r05 -> p10-scorpion (confidence: high).** Pale sandy chitin, six legs spread to the sides, and a long tail that arcs up and over the back, ending in a small dark stinger that points back down. Wrong or missing: the tail is one smooth tube of even thickness with faint bands and no visible segments, and no bulb at the tip, so the stinger is a small dark hook. There are no pincers (not requested, but it makes it read less as a scorpion and more as an elongated bug or louse). The head is a blob with cartoon googly eyes at the front. The legs are identical thin tubes with no readable knees. The body is a smooth sausage. The surface is glossy and plasticky rather than granular chitin.

**r06 -> p16-ant-soldier (confidence: high).** Red glossy chitin, six legs, a three-part body (large round head, small thorax, long oval abdomen) and two dark curved horns on the head. Wrong or missing: the horns (mandibles) hang from the front of the head as two curved black spikes at different angles in the 3/4 view (one long and straight, one hooked) and rise like antennae or horns in the top view, so they do not read as paired jaws that close. There are no antennae. The eyes are small white cartoon eyes with a black pupil, not compound eyes, and there is no visible mouth. The legs are six thin identical tubes with spiky feet. The thorax is small and the waist is not pinched enough to look like an ant's. The abdomen is a plain smooth oval with faint rings.

**r07 -> p18-cobra (confidence: medium-high).** Brown with dark bands, and the front quarter rears up on a vertical column (87 cm of a 2.2 m snake) with a head showing fangs and a yellow slit-pupil eye. Wrong or missing: no hood. The neck is a thick rigid sausage that swells in the middle (front view: a spindle shape), while the rest of the body is a very thin whip-like string, so the proportions look like a caterpillar or worm and not a cobra. It does not curve into an S or flare. The head is small and rounded on top of the fat column. The dark bands are good and even but are plain stripes rather than a banded cobra pattern. The tail section is straight on the ground. The scale texture on the head is good.

**r08 -> p09-long-neck (confidence: high).** A spotted tall grazer, 3.1 m high, with a very long neck, long legs, brown spots on a tan hide and two small horn-like ossicones, with a lizard-like head and a long tail. Wrong or missing: the stance is a running theropod or ostrich (hind legs bent like a bird's, a small hunched body and a long thin pointed tail), not a giraffe-like grazer. The front legs are short and tucked (hard to see in the side view), and all legs are thin and end in thin points without hooves or feet. The neck is thin, uniform and set on the body at a steep, awkward angle. The head is small and slightly crocodile-like with a faint mouth line, and the eyes are small. The spot pattern is good and consistent but made of regular round dots.

**r09 -> p11-sea-serpent (confidence: high).** Dark blue on top, a pale belly (clear on the front of the neck), a row of pale spikes along the whole back and 8.5 m of length, with a big head with fangs and yellow slit eyes. Wrong or missing: the spikes are thin and small for the length, so from the side they read as a fine comb, and they do not grow along the tail. The body is a very long thin tube with no taper character and no tail fin or flippers, so it does not look like it belongs in water. The neck is thick at the head and the head is bulbous; the pale belly shows only on the neck and is hard to see in the long views. The scales are only visible in the close-up. The mouth is a wide line of small teeth with two long fangs.

**r10 -> p14-leopard-stalker (confidence: high).** A tan cat with dark rosettes and spots, white underside, a long ringed tail that curls up at the tip, and a visible slit-pupil green-yellow eye. Wrong or missing: the head is too large and round (bear- or rodent-like), with a short blunt muzzle, tiny ears and a black ring mark on the cheek that looks odd. The body is long and narrow with thin, spindly legs and a stiff gait, and the spots become blotchy and uneven in places. The tail has dark rings, not spots. The fur is a speckled fuzz at the silhouette and in the front view. There are no whiskers and the mouth line is crude.

**r11 -> p03-horn-beetle (confidence: high).** Glossy black, with orange spots on the back, six legs, a small body (31 cm) and one big horn rising from the head and curving up. Wrong or missing: the body is a flat elongated oval like a tick or a ladybird and not a domed beetle, with no wing-case seam, no separate pronotum and a tiny head. The horn is a thin curved spike rather than a heavy forked rhinoceros-beetle horn. The legs are thin, spidery tubes with a very wide splay in the front view. The eyes are a tiny orange dot and there are no antennae or mandibles. The spots are large and sparse, in two uneven columns in the top view. Nothing says "skittish" in the pose.

**r12 -> p19-rhino (confidence: high).** Grey scaly hide, a big horn on the nose and a smaller horn behind it, a 3.8 m body on four columnar legs. Wrong or missing: the body is a plain tube of the same thickness from shoulder to rump, with no shoulder hump, no neck folds and no armour plates; the scales look like pangolin or chain mail and not rhino plates. The head is small for the body and runs straight into it, with no mouth or lip detail and a tiny eye. The legs are thick pillars ending in round feet with three pale nubs. The tail is a thin spike. The ears are small cones. The front horn is good and large and curves back, and the second horn is clear.

**r13 -> p15-gecko (confidence: medium-high).** A low, wide body (21 cm tall, 56 cm wide), four splayed legs with a clear sprawl in the front view, five toes on each foot (clearly countable in the top view), big yellow eyes with slit pupils and a mottled grey-green skin. The body is rounded and bulky like a newt or frog and not truly flat. The head is a smooth blunt dome with a small smile and no nostrils or ridges. The tail is short and tapers evenly. The mottling is camouflage blotches rather than a gecko pattern. The rival is r16 (sprawl lizard); r13 is larger and has the toes and wide body.

**r14 -> p17-goblin (confidence: high).** A small (97 cm) upright figure with a big round head, big yellow eyes with black pupils, green mottled skin, pointed ears and small fangs. **r15 -> p04-green-serpent (confidence: high).** Green with thin yellow stripes, a pale yellow belly, a big head with two long cream fangs hanging from the upper jaw, 6.3 m long. **r17 -> p02-spiked-lizard (confidence: high).** A heavy, low armoured lizard (3.2 m) with a row of pale spikes along the spine, scaled hide, short thick legs with claws and a croc-like head with a row of teeth. **r18 -> p13-cave-bear (confidence: medium-high).** A bulky brown hide 1.3 m high on thick columnar legs, with huge dark curved claws on every foot and tiny eyes (nearly invisible). **r19 -> p20-nightmare-hound (confidence: high).** Black skin with a faint brown grime gradient, glowing red eyes, large white fangs and a row of jagged spines from the neck down the back and out along the tail. **r20 -> p12-raptor (confidence: high).** A two-legged green theropod with dark stripes, a horizontal body, a long thin stiff tail that tapers evenly (the tail is about half of the 3.2 m), small arms held to the chest and a long neck.
