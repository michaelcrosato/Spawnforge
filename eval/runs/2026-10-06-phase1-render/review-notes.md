## Most important problems across all images (most severe first)

1. **Heads and faces are the weakest part.** Heads are spheres or blobs with no snout, muzzle, jaw structure, ears or nose. Mouths are a painted zigzag band or floating white shards. Eyes are cartoon white balls with small pupils. This is why the wolf, hound, bear, boar, troll, goblin and demon feel generic.
2. **Missing identity features.** Several prompt nouns are absent or wrong:
   - wolf: no fur, no ears, no bushy tail
   - cobra: no hood
   - sea serpent: nothing sea-like
   - scorpion: no pincers or segments
   - ant: no antennae
   - troll: tusks placed as horns
   - ram demon: horns lie flat and curl forward, nothing demonic
   - gecko: no wide flat body and no big toe pads
   - hound: not canine
3. **Limbs are thin rods or sausages with no joints, hands, feet or hooves.** Examples are the stilt legs on the wolf and long-neck, thin ant and beetle legs, rubber arms on the troll and goblin, and stub feet on the rhino and boar. Legs are also misplaced on the beetle, where all six sit in the front third.
4. **Surfaces do not carry the material.** Fur, scales, chitin, armour and grime are missing or read as camouflage blotches or cracks (r01, r03, r15, r16). Voronoi crackle looks like dried mud. Black or dark-brown creatures (r10, r20) lose all form to dark shading. Spots on r08 are only readable in the head view.
5. **Body shapes are too tubular and generic.** There is no neck, shoulder, hip or chest definition. Many bodies are capsules or balloons (r04, r07, r14, r19), so unrelated prompts share the same silhouette (r05 / r09 / r19 are near identical apart from colour).
6. **Sheet and camera problems.**
   - The head close-up often crops the head (r01, r02, r03, r04, r08, r10, r16).
   - Top views of upright creatures show only the head (r06, r14, r15).
   - Long thin creatures (r05, r08) are tiny in the side and top panels.
   - The header "x m long" is meaningless for upright creatures.
   - The ground plane has odd curved horizon edges.
   - The rear 3/4 view adds little.
7. **Mouth cut and teeth artifacts.** Stair-stepped jaw edges (r01, r19), teeth that float or detach (r01, r10), and fangs rendered as huge flat triangles (r05).
