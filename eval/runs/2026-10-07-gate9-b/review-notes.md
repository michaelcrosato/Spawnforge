# Gate 9 blind review notes (suite B)

This was a blind pass over r01–r20, matched one-to-one to the 20 prompts in `blind/prompts.md`. I judged only the six-view contact sheets against the prompt text.

**r01 -> b07-reef-shark (confidence: high).** A grey torpedo body with a white belly, a tall triangular dorsal fin, a tall two-lobed tail fin, two pectoral fins and a smaller pair behind them, and a row of teeth along the lips in the head view. Nothing else in the set is a fish. Wrong or missing: the head is a blunt, rounded dome with a terminal mouth and cartoon eyes set on top, not a shark's pointed snout over an underslung mouth. The front view shows a perfectly round cross-section, so it reads as a tube. The teeth are one row of small, square, molar-like pegs that look like a grin, not "rows" of triangular teeth. The pectoral fins have frilly, rayed trailing edges like a bony fish's fins, and from the front they are flat horizontal sticks. The dorsal and tail fins are dark and semi-transparent with a mesh texture, so they nearly vanish against the background. In the top view the dorsal fin shows as a wide wedge off the midline, so it seems to lean sideways. The body pinches at mid-length, and the pelvic fins leave small dark patches on the belly in the side view.

**r02 -> b19-armoured-burrower (confidence: high).** A squat, round body under overlapping transverse bands of armour, a head shield, a ringed tail and huge black claws on the forefeet. This is clearly the armadillo-like digger. Wrong or missing: the bands are flat sheets with stepped, jagged edges that overlap unevenly (the side view looks like stacked shingles), and they stop partway down the flanks, so the shell sits on top like a cape over a large pale body. In the front view a dark slab runs under the belly, which looks like a band crossing through the underside. The head shield has two notched points that read as ears or spikes. The body is very round and tall for a burrower, and the hind feet are small and plain beside the oversized front claws.

**r03 -> b08-cave-spider (confidence: high).** Eight legs, a large glossy rounded abdomen, a narrow cephalothorax with small eye dots, and two fangs hanging down in front. Pale, mottled colouring suits a cave dweller. Wrong or missing: the fangs are bright leaf-green and hang straight down like tusks or blades of grass, so they don't read as venomous chelicerae. The front leg pair is dark brown while the other six legs are pale, which looks like a material mistake. The legs barely arch above the body: in the side view they drop almost straight down like an ant's or a beetle's, without the high knees of a spider. The material is very glossy, almost plastic, with blown-out highlights, and seam lines cross the abdomen. The cephalothorax looks segmented, like an insect thorax.

**r04 -> b10-centaur (confidence: high).** A four-legged body with small dark hooves, an upright torso in front with two arms and hands, and a head with large sideways bull horns. Wrong or missing: the "horse-like" body is a smooth sausage with no withers, chest or croup, and the legs are thick, lumpy tubes without a horse's joints. The hooves are tiny dark nubs. The torso is a featureless pale cylinder with no shoulders, no neck and no chest shape. The arms hang from low on the torso and end in splayed hands. The head is small and frog- or lizard-like with a wide mouth, not human-like. The tail is a stiff dark cone pointing down and back rather than a horse's tail. The join where the torso meets the horse body is a visible lump.

**r05 -> b03-giant-bat (confidence: high).** A dark brown, furry creature with very large pointed ears, small fangs and folded leathery wings along its sides, about 1.5 m long. Wrong or missing: it stands upright on two hind legs set mid-body, like a hamster with wings. The folded wings never act as forelimbs (a bat on the ground props on its wrists), and the folded forearm is a stiff cylinder lying along the back. The wing fingers stick out far past the tail as a ragged fringe of spars. Edge-on, the membrane looks like a bundle of sticks rather than leathery skin. The ears are smooth, flat paddles with no fur and no inner ear. The head merges straight into the body with no neck and no bat muzzle or nose.

**r06 -> b04-hydra (confidence: high).** A heavy four-legged body with a tail and five long necks, each with a toothed reptile head. It is the only five-headed creature in the set. Its rival, r11, has three heads. Wrong or missing: the necks are straight, thick columns of the same length that fan out from one point on the chest, so the front view reads as an open hand or glove. They have no S-curves or sway. The heads are small, identical and all face straight ahead, so they don't look like snapping heads. The legs are short and stumpy, and the body has little character between the neck fan and the cone of a tail.

**r07 -> b16-giant-moth (confidence: high).** A furry, fuzzy body, antennae that are feathery in the top view, six legs, and broad brown wings with a mottled pattern and pale spots, about 1.8 m across. Wrong or missing: only two wings show. In the top view the forewings cover the hindwings almost completely (only a sliver shows at one edge), so "four broad wings" is not visible. In the 3/4 view one wing is a blown-out white specular sheet that looks like shiny plastic or metal. In the side view the resting wings are edge-on and almost invisible, and their spars stick out past the abdomen like sticks. The wing pattern has a fine corduroy-like striping across it. The antennae look like smooth, plain tusks in the 3/4 and side views. The head isn't distinct from the thorax, and the front legs reach forward like arms.

**r08 -> b20-glow-slug (confidence: high).** A long, glossy, dark slate body covered in glowing cyan spots, with two stalks carrying large eyes, about 2.9 m long. Wrong or missing: the eyes are large white cartoon eyeballs with yellow irises, which are cute rather than slug-like. The body is a pointed spindle or lemon shape with no mantle and no flat foot or skirt along the ground, and there are no lower tentacles. The glow pattern looks like a regular dot-matrix grid in places, and in the front view the spots stretch into radial streaks, which shows the projection. The skin is glossy, but nothing marks it as slimy (no wet sheen or trail). The kraken (r17) shares the glowing-spot pattern, but the body plans differ completely.

**r09 -> b18-griffin (confidence: high).** A white-feathered eagle head with a big yellow hooked beak, folded brown feathered wings, bird-like toed feet with claws in front, and a tawny body behind with a tufted lion's tail. Wrong or missing: the beak is enormous and bulbous, close to the size of the skull, so it reads more like a toucan or parrot than an eagle. From the front it is a yellow ball. The folded wings are stacks of blocky, card-like slabs with hard edges that splay out sideways in the front view, rather than layered feathers. The body is one tan colour from neck to tail, so the change from eagle to lion happens only at the white head. The shell fur is noisy and speckled, with a grainy silhouette. The neck is thick and short.

**r10 -> b15-sail-back (confidence: high).** A sprawling, striped reptile with a tall red sail along its back over many spines and a long tail. The frilled lizard (r15) is the obvious rival, but its feature sits at the neck, not on the back. Wrong or missing: the sail reads as a comb of separate red spines with only faint skin between them, and its top edge is ragged and spiky instead of a smooth curve of stretched skin. The spines splay in a fan. The head has two large tusk-like fangs and a dark spike near the back of the skull. The torso is a round barrel in the front view, and the upper limbs are heavy and bulging. The colours are flat: olive skin, dark bands and a red sail.

**r11 -> b05-cerberus (confidence: high).** A black, furry hound with three heads, red eyes, fangs, pointed ears and paws in the front view. Wrong or missing: the outer two heads sit on long necks that splay out about 45 degrees, so it reads like a hydra with three necks rather than a dog with three heads on one broad chest. From the front the outer heads look horse- or seal-like. The all-black coat hides almost all form and detail, so most views are a silhouette. Each head shows one or two large canines rather than a full set of teeth. The tail is a stiff, unfurred cone.

**r12 -> b14-stegosaur (confidence: high).** A large (5.8 m), green, camouflage-patterned quadruped with two staggered rows of tall orange plates along its back (they alternate in the top view), four pale spikes at the tail tip, and white toenails. Wrong or missing: the plates are thin leaf or knife shapes, and from the front they splay out in a V with some tinted purple, so they look like blades stuck in the back. The tail spikes stick out flat sideways, almost horizontal. The head is a smooth, turtle-like dome. The hips aren't noticeably higher than the shoulders, and the back doesn't arch.

**r13 -> b11-sea-turtle (confidence: high).** A domed shell, a green mottled body, long front flippers and short rear flippers, and a beaked head. Wrong or missing: the scutes are a regular grid of rectangles like bricks or barrel staves, not the hexagonal or polygonal scutes of a turtle shell, and the shell is a narrow, elongated oval. The front flippers are straight, flat planks with no curve or taper. The beak is a tan cap over the snout that looks like a separate helmet. The shell sits on the body as a separate cover, with the body skin showing below its rim.

**r14 -> b12-quillback-boar (confidence: high).** A stocky, round brown body with dense pale quills across the back, pale scar stripes on the face and flanks, big white curved tusks, small ears and dark hooves. Wrong or missing: the tusks are very large, horizontal, horn-like sweeps from the sides of the snout, more like a bull's horns or an elephant's tusks than a boar's upturned tusks. The snout is blunt, with no pig nose disc. The quills stick out at random angles, some sideways, like a pincushion, rather than lying back down the spine. The scars are pale, brushstroke-like decals of the same shape repeated. The body is nearly spherical, and I couldn't see a tail.

**r15 -> b13-frilled-lizard (confidence: high).** A slender, mottled lizard with a long banded tail and an orange-red frill of rib-like spokes around the throat and sides of the neck. Its rival is r10, which has a back sail. Wrong or missing: the frill is shown closed in every view and hangs under the jaw like a beard or curtain of separate slats, with little visible membrane, so you can't tell it is a frill that opens. It doesn't wrap over the top of the neck. The upper limbs are heavy and muscular for such a slim lizard, and in the front view it looks like it is doing a push-up. A pale yellow disc on top of the head looks like a stray spot.

**r16 -> b02-wyvern (confidence: high).** A green, horned, long-necked creature standing on two legs, with wings folded at the shoulders and no separate arms, and a small black hooked stinger at the tip of a long tail. The red four-legged dragon (r18) is the rival, and leg count and the stinger settle it. Wrong or missing: the folded wings are hard to read. In the top view the wing arms are two rods lying along the back, and in the front view they hang out sideways as stiff sticks. The membrane is a thin, dark, streaky strip, and it never reaches the ground, so the wings don't look like they double as forelimbs. The stinger is tiny beside the 3.9 m body. The legs are short and set mid-body under a long horizontal body and tail, so the pose looks unbalanced. The face is tube-like, with fangs showing at the corners.

**r17 -> b06-kraken (confidence: high).** A bulbous, dark purple mantle with glowing cyan spots, two dark eyes with yellow slit pupils, and roughly eight long tentacles (counted in the top view), some curling at the tips, 4.1 m long. Wrong or missing: the eyes are not huge. Each is about a tenth of the mantle's width. The tentacles are smooth, uniform tubes with no suckers or taper beyond the tips. In the front view they cross and pass through each other in a tangle. The mantle lies flat on the ground with the tentacles dragging, so nothing suggests swimming in the stills.

**r18 -> b01-dragon (confidence: high).** A red four-legged dragon with a tan belly, black horns swept back over the neck, bat-like wings folded at its sides, and a row of black spikes down the back of its tail. Wrong or missing: the scales are almost invisible. The skin reads as smooth, glossy red plastic, with only faint scale texture on the neck in the close-up. The folded upper wing bone stands up from each shoulder as a tall cylinder. With the membrane edge-on, the wings look like jagged combs of finger spars sticking out past the body, rather than leathery wings. The legs are short and stumpy for a 4.6 m body, and the neck is long and thin, which gives it a weasel-like silhouette. The tail spikes are small and start only behind the hips.

**r19 -> b17-two-tailed-fox (confidence: high).** An orange fox with a white chest and belly, dark lower legs, pointed ears, paws, and two bushy tails that fork apart (clear in the top and rear views). Wrong or missing: the two tails grow from one shared base and read as one forked tail rather than two separate tails. The head is a long, flat wedge with a wide mouth lined with small teeth, more crocodile than fox. The legs are long and thin, and the body is narrow. The fur shell is noisy, with a speckled, blurry outline, and the ears are smooth cones with no fur. From the front the upright tails look like wings or large ears behind the head.

**r20 -> b09-scorpion-king (confidence: high).** Six walking legs (three pairs in the top view), a pair of arms ending in large two-fingered pincers, a stinger tail curled up and forward over the body, a row of tan armour plates down the back, and a crown of yellow spikes on the head that fits "king". Wrong or missing: the tail is a smooth tube with yellow bands rather than a chain of segments, and the stinger at its tip is small and blunt. The armour is a single strip of plates on top, and the sides and underside of the body are bare, so it doesn't look armoured overall (from the front it's a flat tan fan). The pincers are huge and the walking legs thin and spindly. The head has a wide, smiling, almost human mouth. The whole body is glossy brown plastic.
