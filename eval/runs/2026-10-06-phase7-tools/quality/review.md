# Quality review

Each pair shows the same creature drawn two ways, A and B, in random order. For each pair,
pick the better creature, or "same" when you cannot tell them apart or neither is better,
judging by this rubric (in order of weight):

1. silhouette: reads as the creature at a glance, from every view
2. anatomy: believable masses, joints and proportions for its build
3. extremities: feet, hands, claws, horns and tails attach and end well
4. mouth and eyes: placed, shaped and alive
5. surface: skin, patterns and materials read clearly without noise

Write `quality-answers.json` beside this file:
`{ "p01": { "pick": "A", "why": "B's legs pass through the belly" }, … }`.
