# Gate 9 blind review notes (suite A)

Blind pass over r01–r20 (six-view contact sheets, no names), matched one-to-one to the 20 prompts in `blind/prompts.md`.

**r01 -> p19-rhino (confidence: high).** Grey hide with a clear overlapping-scale texture, a large tan horn on the tip of the nose and a second, smaller horn behind it, heavy barrel body on short legs, thin short tail. Nothing else in the set has two nose horns. Wrong or missing: the second horn is not much smaller than the first and sits almost at eye level, and two dark cone "ears" on top of the head read as a third and fourth horn in the 3/4 and front views. The head has no real shape of its own: it widens straight into a huge neck, so from the front the creature is a round ball with a horn stuck in it. The body is a long, even sausage (3.0 m long, 1.1 m tall) with bulges at shoulder and hip, closer to a hippo or a tapir than a rhino's deep chest and hump. The scales look like a pangolin's or a fish's rather than rhino hide with folds. The legs are stubby and end in rows of white nail beads that look like teeth.

**r02 -> p11-sea-serpent (confidence: high).** Long limbless body (5.1 m) that is dark navy on top and pale grey-white underneath, a dense row of pale spikes from behind the neck to near the tail tip, and a dark paddle at the tail end. Matches the colours and spikes as asked. Wrong or missing: the spikes are thin, pale, evenly spaced needles packed like a comb, so they read as a fin fringe or bristles rather than spikes, and they stop short of the head, so the row does not cover "its whole back". The head is a smooth cartoon dome with a wide grin and no fangs or sea features (no fins at the head, no gill slits). From the front the neck flares into a wide bell shape right behind the head, so the head looks perched on a trumpet. The head close-up shows stair-stepped banding in the specular highlight on the skull. The body lies dead straight on the ground, with no undulation that would suggest swimming.

**r03 -> p18-cobra (confidence: high).** Snake, brown with regular dark bands, with the front of the body reared up about 1.1 m and a hood behind the neck; the rear 3/4 view even shows two eye-spots on the back of the hood. Clearly the rearing snake. Wrong or missing: the rear-up is a hard 90° elbow. The body lies flat, then the neck rises as a vertical pipe with no S-curve, so it looks like a lamp post. The hood does not flare from the neck just below the head. It is a separate flat, pale, leaf-shaped sheet hanging mid-neck, much lower than the head, without the body's bands, so in the front view it looks like a pair of wings or a shield behind a pole. It is invisible edge-on in the side view. The head is a small round knob with white cartoon eyes and no visible fangs, and it is not the broad, flattened cobra head.

**r04 -> p04-green-serpent (confidence: high).** Giant (6.7 m) green snake with yellow markings and a pale belly, and two long ivory fangs hanging in front of a row of small teeth. Colours and fangs match. Wrong or missing: the yellow markings are mostly crosswise rings and broken bands, not lengthwise stripes. The fangs hang outside the closed mouth from the lip, parallel and stiff, and the row of small teeth along the whole lip line looks like a zipper. From the front the head is a flat lid on a much wider, round neck bulge (64 cm wide), so the snake looks like a ball with a face. The body lies perfectly straight and flat with no coils or curves, which undersells "giant serpent". It reads as a long tube.

**r05 -> p07-sprawl-lizard (confidence: medium).** Small four-legged lizard (45 cm) with a whip-thin tail longer than the body, pale yellow spots along the back and flanks, and elbows out in the front view. Rival: p15-gecko, which went to r13 because r13 has the flat body and big toes and this one has the spots. Wrong or missing: 45 cm does not read as "tiny", and nothing in the views gives a small sense of scale. The legs are long, thin and nearly straight, so the body stands high on stilts like a spider or a stick insect, especially the hind legs in the side view. That is not a low sprawl. The trunk is pencil-thin next to the head. In the head close-up the spots are huge flat discs, far larger than on the back, and one surrounds the eye like a monocle. The forelegs come out directly under the jaw with no neck, and the toes are tiny threads.

**r06 -> p16-ant-soldier (confidence: high).** Glossy red ant with a large spherical head, two antennae, six legs, a thin waist and a large gaster, and two dark curved mandibles under the head. Matches the body plan and colour. Wrong or missing: the mandibles are thin dark hooks that lie flat along the lower edge of the head and curve sideways, and from the front they read as a moustache rather than two horns jutting forward. The head is a perfect ball with round white cartoon eyes, which makes it cute, not a soldier. There are no compound eyes, ridges or armour. The faint dark lines on the body look like seams. The legs are long, thin and splayed so wide in the front view that the ant looks like it is doing the splits.

**r07 -> p13-cave-bear (confidence: high).** Bulky brown quadruped with grimy, mottled darker blotches, pinpoint eyes and big pale claws on every foot. Bulk, colour, tiny eyes and claws all match. Wrong or missing: the head is long, narrow and tapering like a rat's, an opossum's or a crocodile's, nothing like a bear's broad skull and short muzzle, and small fangs poke out of the lip line. From the front, the small head on an enormous round torso is badly out of proportion. The claws are fanned out like white petals or a feather duster rather than curving down into the ground, and the hind-foot claws are much smaller. The hide shows a polygonal cracked-leather texture in the close-up, with no fur, which "hide" allows, but a cave bear is expected to look shaggy.

**r08 -> p12-raptor (confidence: high).** Biped with a horizontal body, a long tail held straight back, small arms tucked under the chest, green with dark bands and a pale belly, yellow feet. Clearly the raptor. Wrong or missing: the neck rises steeply and the head sits high, and the head is a blunt, round, snake-like knob with a short mouth and tiny teeth, not a long narrow predator's snout. No sickle claw is visible. From the front the torso is a wide bell or cape shape with the thighs bulging outward and a tiny head on top, and it looks wrong. The legs are thin straight sticks with little thigh muscle for a runner.

**r09 -> p02-spiked-lizard (confidence: high).** Low, wide, heavy quadruped lizard (78 cm wide) with a row of pale spikes down the back and a swollen club at the end of a short tail. Body plan and spikes match. Wrong or missing: the "club" is a smooth inflated bulb nearly as thick as the body, with almost no tail before it, so in the rear views it looks like a second body segment or a balloon, not a club on a thick tail. It has no knobs or spikes. Nothing reads as armour. The skin is smooth olive with fine scale texture and has no plates, osteoderms or banding, and the spikes alone carry the "armoured" idea. The head is a broad toad head with a fringe of tiny teeth all round the lip.

**r10 -> p05-swamp-troll (confidence: high).** Upright biped (1.9 m) with mottled green camouflage skin, a flat, wide, frog-like head and two curved white tusks at the corners of the mouth. Matches colour, flat head and tusks. Wrong or missing: the arms are not long. They reach roughly the hips, and in the side view one is held forward at the waist like a zombie. The posture is broken: the side view shows an S-bend with the head craned up and back, and the legs are short, knock-kneed and crossed in the 3/4 view, as if it is tiptoeing. There is no neck, and the head continues straight up out of the torso, so the silhouette is a sock or a slug standing on end. It does not have a troll's hulking shoulders and stooped back.

**r11 -> p01-wolf (confidence: high).** Grey furred quadruped with a long snout, upright pointed ears and a thick tail, lean body (2.1 m). Matches the wolf. Wrong or missing: the paler belly barely reads, and the underside is the same grey or only slightly lighter. The tail is a thick, blunt, carrot-shaped mass held straight out behind and almost as thick as the hindquarters, so in the side and rear views it looks like a continuation of the body rather than a bushy tail hanging or curving. The fur is noisy and speckled, with dithered, see-through edges that are worst in the front view. The ears are smooth bare grey cones that look pasted on. The legs are thick furry columns, and the neck looks thin next to the chest.

**r12 -> p20-nightmare-hound (confidence: high).** Black quadruped hound with glowing red eyes, a row of dark spines from neck to tail, a cluster of big white fangs, and brownish lower legs that could be grime. Matches. Wrong or missing: the spines are regular, evenly curved cones, not jagged, and black on black, so they lose contrast except in silhouette. The grime is barely visible, only a slight brown tint low on the legs. The face is cat-like, with cat ears and a short round head from the front. The fangs are bunched together at the front like a broken fence rather than set as canines. The tail is a thin pointed rat or lizard tail that does not suit a hound.

**r13 -> p15-gecko (confidence: high).** Flat, low lizard (13 cm tall, 36 cm wide across the splayed legs), elbows and knees out sideways, and five long spread toes on every foot. Big bulging eyes on top of the head, mottled olive back and a banded tail. Rival: p07-sprawl-lizard, but the flat stance and big toes are this prompt's distinctive features. Wrong or missing: the toes are long round fingers with no broad adhesive pads, and the forefeet look like human hands in the head close-up. The trunk is narrow from above, so the width comes from the leg span rather than a flat, wide body. The eyes are white balls sitting on top of the head, and the mouth is a shallow smile line.

**r14 -> p10-scorpion (confidence: high).** Sandy, pale tan body with brown bands, six legs, and a long tail curved up and over the back with a dark stinger. Matches. Wrong or missing: the tail is enormous, roughly twice the body length, rising in a tall arch (the creature is 79 cm tall), with the stinger hanging down in front of the head rather than poised over the back. It is a smooth tube with faint stripes and no segments. The body is a soft, glossy, slug-like capsule with no plates or chitin edges, and the gloss reads as plastic. The head is a rounded blob with cartoon eyes and two small brown fangs.

**r15 -> p14-leopard-stalker (confidence: high).** Orange-tan furred cat with dark leopard spots (rosettes on the back from above), pale belly, green slit-pupil eyes in the close-up, and a long banded tail that curls up at the tip. Matches every point. Wrong or missing: the head is blunt and boxy like a pug's or a toad's with a wide grin mouth, without a cat's muzzle and whisker pads. The ears are smooth unfurred ochre cones. The legs are thick and heavy-jointed, and from the front the chest is broad and gorilla-like, so it reads lumbering rather than stalking. The fur is grainy and speckled at the silhouette, and the face spots are plain black blobs.

**r16 -> p03-horn-beetle (confidence: high).** Small (27 cm) glossy black beetle with orange spots, six legs, and one long curved horn rising forward from the head. Matches. Wrong or missing: the body is a single smooth egg with no split between the wing cases, no pronotum and almost no head, so it reads more as a tick or a spider abdomen with a horn attached. The eyes are nearly invisible. The legs are thin black tubes without visible feet segments. The orange spots are flat, evenly sized dots scattered uniformly, including onto the horn base. "Skittish" cannot be judged from stills.

**r17 -> p17-goblin (confidence: high).** Small upright biped (91 cm) with a big round head, big yellow eyes, green mottled skin and long pointed ears. Matches. Wrong or missing: the body is a stiff tube with no shoulders, waist or hips, and the arms are short thin sticks hanging at the sides. The knees are bent in a slight crouch that looks unstable. The teeth are scattered white specks along the mouth that look like flecks of paint. The two ears differ between views, with one long ear and the other often hidden or showing as a small nub, which looks asymmetric. From the front the ears are edge-on and show as tiny pale spikes.

**r18 -> p06-ram-demon (confidence: high).** Bipedal demon (2.1 m), red with black stripes, with big cream ram horns curling round the sides of the head, fangs, and clawed hands and feet. Matches. Wrong or missing: the horns are fat, smooth, ridgeless tubes that curl up and round into near-closed loops like headphones, and from above they form a ring round the head. The body is a pear-shaped sack with a bulging belly, and the stripes are even horizontal rings like a bee costume. The posture is odd: deeply bent forward in the side view, knock-kneed and tiptoeing. There is a visible dark zig-zag seam down the front and a wavy line round the waist in the front and 3/4 views, which looks like a mesh or pattern artifact. The head is small next to the horns.

**r19 -> p09-long-neck (confidence: high).** Very long upright neck, long legs, a long tapering lizard tail, brown spots on tan, and a reptile snout with two dark ossicone-like horns and ears. A clear giraffe-lizard. Wrong or missing: the legs are spindly and kinked like a bird's, with the hind legs very long and crossing in the side view, so the stance looks unsteady rather than a grazer's. The body is small. From the front the chest is a round bulb that looks like a pot with the neck rising from it. At 2.2 m tall it is short for a "tall" grazer, and the neck is long but the creature is not imposing. The feet are tiny.

**r20 -> p08-boar (confidence: medium).** Stocky, low brown body on short legs ending in black hooves, tusks, a tiny upturned stub of a tail, and a row of small dark bristle-spikes along the spine. Rival: p19-rhino, because from the side a long straight white spike juts forward from the snout like a nose horn, but r01 fits the rhino far better (grey scaly hide, horn plus a smaller horn behind). Wrong or missing: the tusk layout is confusing. A long straight tusk points forward and outward and a second curved one hooks down, so from the side it reads as a horn and from the front as walrus tusks sticking out sideways. The head is not notably big and blends into a hippo-like body. There is no pig snout with a nose disc or nostrils, only a flat, rounded wedge. The hide is smooth with a cracked polygon texture and no bristly fur, and the dorsal spikes look like a lizard's crest rather than a boar's mane.
