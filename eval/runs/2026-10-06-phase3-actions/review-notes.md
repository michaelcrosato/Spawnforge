## Most important problems across all images (most severe first)

1. **Limbs and extremities are unfinished.** Legs are plain tubes or pillars without paws, feet, toes or hands (r01, r02, r09, r16, r18, r19, r07). Claws, toes and pincers are thin hooks or flat fans (r02, r10, r07). Hands are missing on every upright creature.
2. **Heads fused to torsos.** Most creatures have no neck or shoulder. The head is a bulge on a sack (r01, r16, r18, r19, r13). Upright biped posture is a hunched, backward-leaning sack with thin limbs (r16, r18, r19).
3. **Googly-eye default.** Almost every creature has the same oversized spherical eye with a slit pupil, whatever the prompt asked for ("tiny eyes" on the bear still gets pinprick dots). Eyes dominate close-ups (r06, r10, r05).
4. **Surfaces do not match the prompt.** No fur anywhere (wolf, bear, boar, leopard); the "fur" is a cloudy camouflage blotch. The mottle reads as military camo on the troll and goblin. The crazed scale texture shows aliased, stair-stepped speculars in close-ups (r03, r12, r15, r17). Texture seams are visible (r04 neck dots, r19 belly diagonal).
5. **Prompt features wrong or missing.** Cobra (r03) does not rear and has no real hood, only a fat egg and a pencil tail. Club tail (r12) is thin before the club and looks like a second head. Boar has unrequested back spikes (r01). Long-neck (r04) lacks visible front legs and reads as an ostrich. Gecko (r07) is thin, not flat and wide, with small toes. Wolf (r13) has no paler belly. Nightmare hound spines are red, not black. Ant has no antennae. Scorpion has no real pincers.
6. **Teeth and small details are floating specks.** White flecks on the snout of r02, r07, r13 are not rows of teeth seated in a mouth. Fangs on r17 sit outside the mouth.
7. **Render framing.** Long creatures (snakes, lizards, sea serpent) are tiny in the 3/4 and rear panels. Head close-ups are often cropped, with the head half out of frame (r01, r02, r12) or showing only a blob (r10). Scale bars are fine.
8. **Body structure is generic.** Many bodies are smooth sausages or barrels with little anatomy: no segmentation (beetle, scorpion, ant), a plain tube torso (leopard, wolf), and spine rows that look the same across species.
