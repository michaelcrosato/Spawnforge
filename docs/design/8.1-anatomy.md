# Design: muscle masses, joints and body shape (milestone 8.1)

Every plan 1 review said the bodies read as tubes: legs of even width from hip to foot, no
chest, no hips, rods for tails. 8.1 gives every body anatomy from rules, scaled by `muscle`, so
any blueprint (and phase 9's bodies) gets it without new fields. This design was reviewed before
building; see [Review](#review).

## Today

- The skin is a signed distance field (`compile/sdf.ts`): one rounded cone per skin bone (or one
  per span of its radius profile), with an elliptical cross-section. Bones of a chain join with a
  plain union; a chain joins its parent with a smooth minimum (`chain.blend`).
- Each limb chain has one extra sphere at its root (radius 1.12 × the root radius), the
  "shoulder and hip masses", also in a plain union.
- Skin weights come from each bone's own rounded cone (`skin.ts`, `boneDistanceXYZ`): nearest
  bone, its parent and children, softmax of distance over radius.
- Parts find the surface by marching the field (`parts.ts`), so they follow any new shape.
- `analyze` measures mass from the skin mesh's volume (follows the shape), but checks limb
  intersections and builds hit capsules from `bones.radii`, the cone radii.

## The rules

All sizes scale with the local radius `r` (the bone's radius where the mass sits) and with
`s = 2 × muscle` (0 to 2; the default muscle 0.5 gives s = 1). Every mass and every profile
change is a function of `s` that vanishes at s = 0, so `"muscle": 0` stays close to today's
tubes. No random draws: the same blueprint gets the same body.

### Masses: oriented ellipsoids owned by bones

A mass is `{ bone, center, axes (along, side, up), radii (along, side, up), blend }`, in the
bone's frame (`up` is the limb's front face, the section's dorsal side on the main axis). It
joins its chain with a smooth minimum of radius `blend` (about 0.5 r), not a plain union, so it
swells the surface instead of sitting on it.

**Limbs** (`leg` and `arm` roles; segment `k` of `n`; materials other than chitin):

| Where | Mass | Center | Radii (along, side, up) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limb root (all roles, all materials) | Shoulder or hip, replacing today's sphere | 0.2 r_root along the first bone | (1.15, 1.1 + 0.08 s, 1.1 + 0.12 s) × r_root |
| Thigh / upper arm (k = 0), front | Quadriceps / biceps | t = 0.5, +0.22 s r toward the face | (0.36 ℓ, 0.85 + 0.12 s, 0.7 + 0.2 s) × r for side and up |
| Thigh / upper arm (k = 0), back | Hamstring / triceps | t = 0.35, −0.25 s r | (0.32 ℓ, 0.85 + 0.1 s, 0.7 + 0.22 s) × r |
| Lower leg (k = 1), hind legs and bipeds | Calf, behind the shin | t = 0.3, −0.3 s r | (0.3 ℓ, 0.8 + 0.12 s, 0.65 + 0.25 s) × r |
| Lower leg (k = 1), front legs of quadrupeds / forearm | Forearm belly, near the elbow | t = 0.25, centred | (0.3 ℓ, 0.9 + 0.15 s, 0.85 + 0.12 s) × r |
| Joint between k and k + 1 (all joints but the ankle or wrist of the last segment) | Knee or elbow cap | the joint, 0.35 r_j toward the outside of the bend | (0.45, 0.5, 0.45) × r_j, times (0.6 + 0.2 s) |

`ℓ` is the segment's length. "Toward the face" is +up; the outside of a bend is the opposite of
the bisector of the two bones at the joint (so elbows point back and knees forward whichever
way a leg bends); a joint bent less than 15° gets no cap. Segments past the second (k ≥ 2:
metatarsals, hands) get no belly. Every mass belongs to the bone it sits on (caps to the lower
bone of their joint).

**Torso** (when the body has limbs, and its material is not chitin):

| Mass | Where | Radii, × the torso radius r_t there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chest (ribcage) | At the frontmost limb pair's `at` + 0.08 (toward the tail), 0.12 s r_t toward the belly | along 0.24 × torso length; side 1 + 0.1 s; up 1 + 0.14 s |
| Pelvis and hips | At the hindmost leg pair's `at`, 0.1 s r_t toward the back | along 0.18 × torso length; side 1 + 0.08 s; up 0.95 + 0.08 s |
| Shoulder blades (quadrupeds: two or more leg pairs, the front pair) | Above each front leg's root: 0.55 r_t dorsal and 0.4 r_t to that side | (0.14 × torso length, 0.32 r_t, 0.16 r_t) × (0.7 + 0.3 s); owned by the leg's first bone, so they slide with the stride |

A body with one limb pair (a biped, or arms only) has a chest at that pair and a pelvis at its
legs; with no legs, no pelvis; with no limbs (serpents), no torso masses.

**Neck**: a mass at the base of the first neck bone, t = 0.15, 0.25 s r_n toward the back, long
along the neck (0.5 ℓ, (1 + 0.15 s) r_n, (0.9 + 0.2 s) r_n): the muscle running into the
shoulders. The neck's junction blend grows by 25% × s.

### Profiles

Profile multipliers act on the radii the blueprint gives (each bone gets 4 to 6 profile points),
so they show in the cones themselves:

- **Joints**: each limb segment narrows over its last 30% to 1 − 0.12 s, and the second
  segment's far end (ankle, wrist) to 1 − 0.2 s.
- **Chitin limbs** instead swell in the middle and narrow at both ends: × (1 − 0.15 s + 0.3 s ·
  sin(π t)), and get no bellies or caps (insect legs are segments, not muscle).
- **Waist**: with a chest and a pelvis, the torso narrows halfway between them by 0.08 s.
- **Tails** start muscular: × (1 + 0.25 s × (1 − smoothstep(0, 0.35, t))) from the root.

### S-curved neck

`neck.curve` (degrees) makes the neck a cubic Bézier: the base control point is pushed forward
and down, the head-end one back and up, along the neck's normal, by an amount that grows with
the curve, so the neck leaves the torso forward and rises under the head like a swan's. The
head's own pitch still sets where it looks. 0 keeps today's curve.

## What follows the masses

- **The field** (`sdf.ts`): a new primitive kind, the ellipsoid, with Inigo Quilez's bounded
  approximation (`k0 (k0 − 1) / k1`), its three axes in the slots a cone keeps for its frame. A
  chain's masses fold into its bones' plain union with a smooth minimum, in a second pass after
  the cones, in index order, so evaluation stays deterministic whatever primitives a grid block
  sees. Mass blend radii count in `maxBlend`, so block culling keeps every mass that can touch a
  block.
- **Skin weights** (`skin.ts`): a bone's distance is the nearer of its cone and its masses, so a
  calf behind the shin, a cap or a shoulder blade weights to its owner, not to whichever bone
  axis happens to be close.
- **Radii** (`bones.radii`, read by the limb-intersection checks and hit capsules): the largest
  of the cone radius and each owned mass's reach from the axis, so checks and capsules cover
  the muscle.
- **Parts** re-seat by themselves, since sockets march the field; sockets that read bone radii
  (claws, tips) are unchanged.
- **Mass and centre of mass** come from the skin volume already.

## Controls

`body.muscle` (0 to 1, default 0.5) and `limbs[].muscle` (default: the body's) are 7.3's fields;
8.1 builds them and `neck.curve`, so their `notBuilt` rows and the guide's "Not drawn yet" rows
go. Nothing new enters the format.

## Bodies of phase 9

The rules key on role, segment and attachment, not on body plans. Wings (9.3) are arm-like: the
root mass becomes the flight muscle, and the chest rule (at the frontmost limbs) gives the keel.
Fins get root masses only. Tentacles get the tail's muscular base. Extra heads' necks (9.1) each
get the neck mass. Centaurs' upright fronts are arm pairs on a forward neck section and get their
own chest from the arms. Each phase 9 milestone checks its bodies against these rules in renders.

## Checks

- Unit tests on the skeleton: masses per role and segment, none at s = 0, owners, cap direction
  for forward and backward bends, chitin profiles.
- Field tests: an ellipsoid's distance has the right sign and zero set; a mass's smooth union is
  continuous and independent of primitive order and block culling.
- Weights: a vertex on a calf mass weights mostly to the shin.
- `muscle: 0` stays within 3% of today's skin volume for every example.
- Before-and-after sheets of every example; then the 7.5 quality review of the phase 4 run's 20
  blueprints at `d101c06` against this milestone (≥ 16 of 20 prefer the new look); compile time,
  triangles, fuzz, motion tests (no new foot slide or limb intersections, now with honest radii)
  and goldens re-recorded once.
