# Design: tentacles, antennae, mandibles and pincers (milestone 9.4)

Plan 2's 9.4 (effort xhigh): tentacle limbs that curl, sway and reach; part modules that declare
bones of their own (the `bones` hook sketched in [the rig design](rig.md)), so `antenna` is one
file; `mandible` and `hand.pincer`, whose hinged pieces close with the bite or a pinch; and the
`pinch` and `lash` actions. **Done when** kraken-like, moth (antennae), spider and scorpion
examples work with their actions, and the harness covers parts with bones.

## Today

- Format 0.2 already holds the vocabulary: `role: "tentacle"` (`curl`, `curlStart`, up to 16
  `segments`, `foot`), `antenna`, `mandible`, `hand.pincer`, `pinch` (needs `pincer`) and `lash`
  (needs a tail or a tentacle). They validate and are skipped (`notBuilt`).
- `Rig.chains` holds driven chains: tails (`spring`) and eyelids (`blink`, with a `closed`
  pose of turns about each bone's local X). The controller runs springs generically; `applyFace`
  runs jaws and blinks.
- Parts already put bones in the skeleton: eyes (and their lids) and, since 9.3, feathers
  (`ctx.featherBone`). Neither is general.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tentacle skeleton | A chain of `segments` bones from the attach point along a rest curve: straight for `curlStart`, then turning by `curl` degrees in total, evenly per bone, toward the belly (the body's down at the root, carried along). Thick bones are in the SDF; thin ones become swept tubes, as tails' tips do. |
| 2 | Tentacle motion | Each tentacle is a `spring` chain (`stiffness` 0.18, softer than a tail's 0.35) whose rest shape is the curl, so it sways and trails. Its points collide with the ground (they drape rather than sink); tails keep today's behaviour. |
| 3 | Reaching | A new goal, `grab` (0 to 1), bends the tentacles nearest the look target toward it by FABRIK on the spring's points (the spring then relaxes it back). FABRIK suits long chains and keeps segment lengths exact; the neck keeps its CCD. |
| 4 | Parts with bones | `hooks.bones(ctx, params)` returns chains in socket space (points, radii, optional drive, stiffness, poses). Compile appends them after the body's bones, parented to the socket's bone, before `build`; `build` gets them as `ctx.chains` (bone ids and model-space points) and binds pieces to them with per-vertex weights (`ctx.solid` / `emit` with `bone`). Driven chains join `Rig.chains`. |
| 5 | New drives | `jaw` (a pose blended by the jaw opening: mandibles open and close with it) and `grip` (a pose blended by the new `grip` goal: a pincer's finger shuts). Both apply poses as blinks do, turns about each bone's local X. `applyFace` runs `jaw` and `blink`; the controller runs `grip`. |
| 6 | Antenna | One chain of `segments` bones (spring, stiffness from the param) along a rest curve back by `curve`; segments swept rigid on their bones with a short blend at each joint; `club` thickens the tip, `feather` adds comb barbs on the last two thirds. |
| 7 | Mandible | A pair at the mouth corners (mouth slot), each one bone hinged at the corner and driven by the jaw: open spreads them apart, shut crosses their tips. `fang` hangs them down like chelicerae. Teeth along the inner edge. |
| 8 | Pincer | A foot on any limb: a palm on the limb's last bone and two fingers, the fixed one part of the palm, the moving one on a bone of its own driven by `grip`. |
| 9 | `pinch` | Raises the arms toward the target (the `arms` goal), opens the pincers wide, then snaps them shut (`grip` 1) with a `pinch-contact` event. `both` snaps both together; otherwise the nearer one. |
| 10 | `lash` | The tail or tentacle nearest the target whips: a wind-up away, then a strike toward the target along an arc of `arc` degrees, solved by FABRIK on the chain's points, with a `lash-contact` event at the strike's peak. A tail takes the strike from its root; a tentacle from its base. |
| 11 | Suckers | A pattern, `suckers`: rows of pale rings along the underside of limbs (body coordinates `limb` along, `height` round), so they draw on tentacles and anything else with region `limbs`. |
| 12 | Kraken body | No new preset: a `serpent` with a big head, tentacles on the torso at `at` 0.95 ringed round the rear (several entries at different `angle`s), as the format says. Legless bodies with tentacles on the torso are swimmers (`media`), slithering on land until 10.3. |

## Tentacle skeleton (decision 1)

- **Root** on its section (torso, neck, head or tail) at `at`/`angle`, just inside the skin,
  pointing out along the surface normal blended toward the section's back end (60%), so torso
  tentacles trail behind as a squid's do and head tentacles ring the mouth pointing forward and
  down.
- **Curl plane**: spanned by the root direction and the belly direction at the root (the
  section's down, flipped for `curl` < 0). Each bone after the straight `curlStart` share turns by
  `curl / curled bones`. Bone lengths are even; the radius follows the limb's profile.
- **Not legs**: tentacles never bear weight, never change hip height or posture.
- **Rig**: `TentacleRig { id, side, bones, curl }` in `rig.tentacles`, and a `spring` chain.
- **Ground**: a resting tentacle may lie on the ground; compile lifts any joint below the ground
  by bending the chain at its root (the curl is kept), and warns `below_ground` only when the
  root itself is below.
- **Foot**: a `foot` on a tentacle (a club, a pincer) sits on the tip as on any limb.

## Parts with bones (decision 4)

```ts
interface PartChain {
  /** Socket space (+Y out of the skin, +Z forward), root first: one more point than bones. */
  readonly points: readonly Vector3[];
  readonly radii: readonly number[];
  readonly drive?: 'spring' | 'jaw' | 'grip';
  /** Springs: pull back toward rest per step, 0 to 1. */
  readonly stiffness?: number;
  /** jaw and grip: each bone's turn about its local X at full drive (radians). */
  readonly pose?: readonly number[];
}
hooks.bones?(ctx: PartBuildContext, params): readonly PartChain[];
```

- Called per copy of the part (side, instance), after the socket is known and before `build`.
  Bones are named `<part id>.<chain>.<k>` (`antennae.L.0.3`), section `part`, owner the part's
  id, `skin: false`, parented to the socket's main bone. Mirrored copies mirror the points.
- `ctx.chains` in `build`: for each chain, its bone ids and model-space joints, so a piece can be
  swept along it with weights blended at the joints (`ctx.solid` takes weights per vertex).
- Hit capsules, sockets and markers ignore part bones (an antenna is not a hit volume); the
  export carries them, and clips track them like any bone.
- Fingerprints hash bone positions, so an example gains new goldens when a part with bones is
  added to it, and nothing else moves.

## Motion

- **Springs** gain the ground for tentacles (decision 2) and part chains (antennae never sink).
- **`grab`** (decision 3): FABRIK toward the look target over the nearest tentacles' spring
  points, blended by the goal; the spring carries the chain back after. `bite` sets `grab` on
  bodies with tentacles, as it sets `arms` on bodies with arms.
- **`grip`** (decision 5): the controller damps a grip value toward the goal (0.08 s) and poses
  `grip` chains; `pinch` drives it.
- **`lash`** (decision 10): during the action the chosen chain's spring targets follow a strike
  path; the spring's inertia gives the whip.
- Order per step: as today, then tentacle FABRIK after springs, then grips with the face.

## Analysis

- `tentacle_intersection`: tentacle bones passing into legs or the body by more than 1% of the
  torso while walking (tentacles touching each other are fine), with fixes: attach further back,
  shorter, more curl, fewer.
- The description names tentacles (`eight tentacles`), antennae, mandibles and pincers.
- `measurements.reach`: the longest tentacle's length, for games.

## Examples

- `kraken`: serpent body, a broad head with a beak, eight tentacles ringed round the rear, two
  long feeding tentacles with club feet, suckers; `bite` and `lash`.
- `luna-moth` gains feathery antennae (its golden changes).
- `tomb-spider` gains fang chelicerae (its golden changes).
- `scorpion` (new): hexapod-like octopod body, two arms with `hand.pincer`, a tail curled up over
  the back ending in a stinger (`horn.curved` on the tail tip); `pinch` and `lash`.

## Tests

- **Skeleton:** a tentacle with `curl` 270 turns its tip back toward the root; `curlStart` keeps
  that share straight; segments are even; a tentacle on the head rings the mouth.
- **Parts with bones:** the harness builds every part with a `bones` hook with its defaults, its
  example and random parameters, twice the same; its bones are named, parented to the socket's
  bone and skinned; mirrored copies mirror.
- **Drives:** mandibles open with the jaw and close past each other; a pincer finger turns by its
  pose at grip 1; springs keep antennae above the ground.
- **Motion:** tentacles sway and drape (no point below the ground); `grab` brings the nearest
  tentacle's tip within 10% of the target when it is in reach; `pinch` fires `pinch-contact`
  with the pincers shut; `lash` fires `lash-contact` with the tip near the target.
- **Examples:** each compiles within budgets, analyses without intersections, and renders.

## Budgets

Tentacles add up to 16 bones each and a spring each: a kraken with ten tentacles adds about 120
bones and stays within 0.1 ms of motion per creature (springs are a few vector operations per
point). Part chains are a handful of bones.

## Not in 9.4

Swimming with tentacles (10.3), tentacles as legs (an octopus walking on them), suckers as
geometry, ears on springs (the hook allows it; no module asks yet).

## As built

- **Tentacle root and direction**: as decision 1, with the root direction 30% out of the skin and
  70% along the section toward its far end; head tentacles point forward (the first draft took
  every section's back end, which laid head tentacles along the flanks). A joint that would meet
  the ground turns the curl axis vertical, so the rest of the chain lies on the ground and curls
  on across it, away from the midline, rather than bending the chain at its root.
- **Parts with bones**: chains are given in model space, placed with `ctx.toModel(socket, p)`
  (socket space, mirrored for left copies), with an optional `parent` bone (a pincer's finger
  hangs on the limb's last bone) and `up` per chain. Bones are named `<part>.<chain>.<k>`
  (`antennae.L.0.3`, `claw.L.foot.0.0`). `_solid.ts` in the pack sweeps a piece and weights it
  along a chain or onto one bone.
- **Pincer**: the moving finger is hinged on top of the palm's end and closes downward onto the
  fixed finger, crab-style; each finger's last third takes `tipColor`. `grip` is per side in
  `applyFace` (`[left, right]`), so `pinch` without `both` shuts only the claw nearer the target
  (the new `nearest` goal, which `arms` follows too).
- **Arms that reach**: the `arms` goal turned every arm by 1.3 rad, which lifts a claw already
  held forward to vertical. Each arm now turns by `0.62 − its hand's rest elevation`, at most
  1.3 rad: hanging arms (every earlier example) keep 1.3; a scorpion's lifts about 0.8.
- **Lash and grab** bend the spring's rest shape, not its points: a lash rotates the rest chain
  about its root toward the target (at most `lashArc`; winding up turns it half that the other
  way) and, from 60% of the strike, draws the tip onto a target in reach by FABRIK; a grab
  blends the rest shape toward a FABRIK solve. The spring's inertia gives the whip; a lashing
  chain pulls toward its rest at least 0.5 per step. The lash takes the tail or tentacle whose
  tip is nearest the target (not a legless body's main tail, which follows its trail); a grab the
  two nearest tentacles.
- **Ground**: a spring joint below the ground swings up onto it about the joint before, keeping
  the segment's length (clamping its height alone let the posed chain drift under).
- **Suckers** read `height` round each tentacle bone (-1 on the inside of its curl), which
  body coordinates now give tentacles instead of the limbs' flank-and-belly blend.
- **Analysis**: `measurements.tentacleReach` (not `reach`, which is the bite's). The ground
  penetration check now measures a torso by its half-height: a wide or legless torso rests on a
  flattened belly, and the round radius made a kraken's mantle read as 6 cm under the ground.
- **Kraken**: a `serpent` with the tentacles on the head (eight arms and two clubbed feeding
  tentacles, the club made by the `radius` profile; no club foot), a short thick torso as the
  mantle, no tail and no beak: `beak` sheathes the whole front of a round head.
- **Budget**: the kraken first cost 0.24 ms of motion a frame, mostly sampling the terrain under
  every spring point. Each point now resamples the ground only after moving 5% of the torso
  length; springs take one length pass (a second only rounded); the solve before the springs
  skips the springs' own bones; aimed spring bones solve once; and `solvePose` keeps its cached
  bone list for creatures without stations (it rebuilt it every call). The kraken runs at about
  0.08 ms, and every creature with springs got a little faster.
- **Not done**: ears on springs (no module asks yet), suckers as geometry (as planned).
