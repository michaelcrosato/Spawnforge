# Design: variation for the new vocabulary (milestone 9.6)

Phase 5's variation tools (`mutate`, `crossbreed`, `generate`) predate heads, tails, wings, fins,
tentacles and coverings. 7.3 froze every field named `count`, plus `forkAt`, `media` and `area`,
"until 9.6 designs breeding across them". 9.6 says what each tool does with them, adds four
themes, and lets `rpg` read the new bodies.

## Genes

- **Counts of heads and tails** (`body.neck.count`, `body.tail.count`) are not genes. A count is
  structure, not a quantity to blend: no creature has two and a half heads.
- **Every other `count`** is a gene again, such as a row's spikes, a jaw's teeth or a shell's
  scutes. 7.3 froze them by key name, which also froze `spikes.row`'s `count` by accident.
- **`forkAt`** is a number gene. Zero stays zero, as for every number, so a single tail never
  forks by mutation.
- **`media`** stays fixed: media follow the body (wings fly, fins on the torso swim). **`area`**
  stays fixed: it places a covering, as `on` does.

## Mutation

Mutation drifts what a creature has; it never grows something new. So it never changes how many
heads, tails or limbs there are. Wings and heads come from themes, `patch` and crossbreeding.

Structural changes, one at most per child, as before:

- **Add** a part that shares a tag with the creature's parts.
- **Remove** a part.
- **Swap** a part for a module with the same slot and a shared tag: spikes, plates and a sail
  (`back`), a shell and armour bands (`armor`), a frill and a hood (`display`), teeth, a beak and
  mandibles (`mouth`).
  - A swap to a module that sits elsewhere by default takes that module's anchor. A dorsal fin
    swapped to a tail fin moves to the tail tip.
- **Feet**, new: every limb of one role that wears one foot type changes together. A wolf never
  ends up with hooves in front and paws behind.
  - Legs change to feet that stand a leg: a module with a `stance` (paw, hoof, pad, talon), or
    the pack's default leg foot.
  - Arms change to feet without a stance: claws, a grasping hand, a pincer.
  - Wings, fins and tentacles keep theirs.
- Membrane modules belong to limbs, so they are never placed in `parts`.
- A part tagged `aquatic` (fins) is added or swapped in only for a creature that swims. The core
  maps that tag to the water medium, as it reads `sense` for sense organs; it names no module.

## Crossbreeding

The child is built on one parent's body plan, as before. The new rules decide which parent the
new structure comes from.

- **Heads and tails.** Each section's count comes whole from one parent: the base's, or the
  donor's with chance `mix`. That parent's `spread` comes with it, and for tails its `forkAt`.
  - Parts on a head are copied to every head when the creature is expanded, so a child that
    takes five necks gets five fully dressed heads.
  - Parts placed on one instance (`head.R2`) that no longer exists are dropped.
  - A tail count means nothing on a body without a tail, so it is skipped there.
- **Wings, fins and tentacles.** Limbs of these roles pair one to one, by id and then from front
  to back, and blend gene by gene. How many of each role the child has comes from one parent,
  the donor's with chance `mix`:
  - taking the donor's count brings over its unmatched limbs of that role (a wolf grows a
    griffin's wings);
  - or it drops the base's unmatched ones (a griffin built on its own body, at mix 1 toward a
    wolf, loses them).
  - Legs and arms are the base's body plan, so they never come or go.
  - `--lock limbs` keeps the base's limbs as they are.
  - A limb the child gains that does not fit its body is dropped when the child is validated
    (`finish`), as parts are. Limbs the base had are never dropped.
- Each section and role draws once from its own stream, whatever happens. So locking one never
  moves another, and the same seed always gives the same child.

## Generate and themes

The grammar stays one for every theme. A theme only weights choices and narrows ranges.

- **New bias fields:**
  - `limbs`: optional limb sets, each joining with its chance, on some plans only. An item with
    an id the body already has changes that limb, so `{ id: 'foreleg', foot: { type:
    'foot.hoof' } }` puts hooves on a beast.
  - `skin`: skin fields beside the palette, such as fur ranges.
  - Theme shapes may set `motion.media`, for a walker that swims.
- **`requires`** (CLI `--requires air,water`) names media the creature must move in. Media, not
  module ids.
  - **Air** needs wings. Plans that cannot get them in this theme, from their shape or an
    optional limb set, are left out, and an optional wing set is forced in when the drawn body
    has no wings yet.
  - A theme with no winged body fails at once, listing the themes that have wings.
  - **Water** (or land) that the body does not give on its own is switched on in
    `motion.media`, for a walker that paddles (10.3 builds the paddle).
  - Every attempt is checked against the creature's resolved media.
- **Themes:**
  - `dragon`: four legs and wings, or a wyvern. Leathery wings, now and then feathered. Horns,
    crests, tail spikes, rarely a frill.
  - `aquatic`: sharks and fish; finned eels and sea serpents; and crocodile-like walkers that
    swim.
  - `eldritch`: tentacled krakens, two- or three-headed hounds and spiders. Faces of tentacles,
    two more pairs of eyes, slime, veins and a glow.
  - `beast`: muscled furred quadrupeds on paws, or on cloven hooves a third of the time, with
    ears and fangs, and now and then horns, tusks or bristles.
  - Bipeds and centaurs were tried in `eldritch` and `beast` and read as columns at this size,
    so they are left out.
- Each theme renders plausibly across 20 seeds (`node scripts/theme-sheet.ts <theme>`). A test
  checks that every optional part and limb set of every theme turns up across 40 seeds, so a
  typo cannot silently drop one.

## Stats

- **Head per part.** Stats inputs carry `head` for each part on a head, found through its neck,
  its jaw or the part it sits on. Parts on a head are copied to every head, so without it a
  stats module cannot tell five heads' teeth from one head's.
- **`rpg`:**
  - `attack` is one head's hit: weapons on the main head, plus weapons off the heads.
  - The new `attacks` is one a turn per head, and `threat` uses attack × attacks.
  - Defence gains `10 × cover + 0.15 × pieces` for each armour part (tag `armor`): shells,
    bands and plates. Cover is the part's size over the torso length, at most 1.
  - `hide` counts 2, between skin and scales.
  - So the stone tortoise's defence rises from 3 to 17, the plated stegosaur's from 14 to 21,
    and the hydra attacks 5 times a turn.

## Eval

The variation eval gains four tasks over the new vocabulary, and the gate is every task:

- **v09:** a dragon that flies and stands no taller than 1.2 m.
- **v10:** a wolf × griffin cross that keeps the wolf's four paws and grows wings.
- **v11:** three hydra mutants that keep their five heads and gain no wings.
- **v12:** an eldritch creature with two or more heads, armoured to an `rpg` defence of 12.

Runs from before 9.6 are still scored on their eight tasks.
