# Design: flight (milestone 10.4)

Winged creatures take off, fly, glide, hover (insects), bank and land, with any wing 9.3 builds.
This is the third medium after land and water (10.3), and the last new locomotion of plan 2.
Games will code against its API, and the look of flight must hold for an 8 m dragon, a feathered
griffin, a bat and a moth; no test can judge that alone. Hence max effort: the first draft was
reviewed adversarially (31 findings, 3 blockers), and this is the answered design. The findings
and answers are at the end.

Rule one: nothing changes for creatures without wings, and winged creatures on the ground walk
exactly as today (same poses, same analyze warnings) until something asks them to fly.

## What exists

- **Wings (9.3).** Each `WingRig` has its bones (humerus, forearm, hand), digit chains, feather
  bones, the bind-pose plane normal, `span` (root to the farthest bone tip, one wing) and `area`
  (membrane triangles, m²), and the pose `folded` (= rest); bind is spread. `applyWings` slerps
  every wing bone from rest toward bind; stations then carry the membranes exactly between spars.
  The membrane module's `hooks.wing` style (compile time) shapes and folds the wing; `shell`
  marks a beetle's case.
- **Media (10.3).** `medium: 'land' | 'water'`, `enterMedium`, gaits per medium, a pace per
  medium, a `medium` event, and a target height for divers.
- **Leaps (10.2).** A planned ballistic arc with crouch, takeoff, flight and landing.
- **Gait stubs.** `fly` (`stroke`, `bank`), `glide` (needs `glide`; `sink`), `hover` (needs
  `hover`; `rate`), all `medium: 'air'`. Wings default `media.air` to true.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | When does it fly? | Only when asked: `fly()`, a scenario `fly` call, `place(..., { flying })`/`spawn({ flying })`, or a `moveTo` whose `y` is more than 1.5 × its standing height above the ground there. On its own it walks; walking winged creatures are unchanged. |
| 2 | How does the body move? | A point mass with speed, heading and climb rate steered toward a 3D target, with turn and climb limits from bank and power. No aerodynamic integration: speeds, rates and bank follow from wing loading and size (decision 4). Deterministic at the fixed 120 Hz step. |
| 3 | States | `takeoff` → `flight` (air gait `flap`, `glide` or `hover`) → `landing` → land, in a new `air` medium. Gait modules declare `air: 'flap' \| 'glide' \| 'hover'` (as 10.3's `swim`); the core never names `fly`. |
| 4 | Scaling | Computed once at compile, independent of quality, stored in `compiled.motion.flight`: mass (the skin's volume × 1000 kg/m³, as `analyze`), tip-to-tip span `b` (spread bounds, feathers included) and planform area `S` (per lifting wing, the convex hull of its membrane in its bind plane, so overlapping feather ranks count once; cases excluded). Cruise `v = √(2mg / (ρ S C_L))`, ρ 1.225, C_L 1.0. Slow flight `v_min = 0.5 v` (flapping hard), stall for gliders `0.7 v`. Wingbeat `f = m^(3/8) g^(1/2) b^(-23/24) S^(-1/3) ρ^(-3/8)`, shown at most 10 Hz. |
| 5 | Can it fly? | It always can (a game decides what flies); `analyze` warns `cannot_fly` above 700 N/m² wing loading, with the span it would need at that loading, and when `S` is 0 (no lifting wing). |
| 6 | Strokes | Compiled into each `WingRig.stroke` from the membrane style: axes in the body's bind frame (`out`, `lead`, `normal`) with a side sign, amplitude, flex, twist and plane. Applied as a turn of the humerus about `lead` (in the body's current frame, converted into the parent's), a twist about the humerus's own long axis, a blend of the outer wing toward a compiled `flexed` pose on the upstroke, and a stroke plane tilted from `lead` toward `normal`. Centre wings and cases hold a flight pose. |
| 7 | Clearance | Peak elevation (stroke + dihedral) ≤ 80°, downstroke ≥ −60° below the wing plane; bank and amplitude are clamped every step so the lower wingtip (feathers included) clears the floor by 5% of span; the floor is the higher of ground and water surface, sampled under the body, under each tip and along the predicted turning path. The default cruise height is the shoulder height + half the span + 1 m (at least 2 m). |
| 8 | Gliding | `sink` (0–1) sets the glide ratio `18 − 14 · sink` (default 13.8:1). Flap-gliding is a height band: a glider glides while at or above its asked height and flaps (climbing gently) when it falls 3% below, so level flight stays within the 10% tolerance. |
| 9 | Landing | Planned like a leap: the touchdown point, an approach at slow-flight speed descending at up to 12°, and a flare of `0.8 s × timeScale` decelerating to walking pace (up to 3 g, wings as air brakes), all checked against the floor at 16 points and kept a leg's reach above it until the flare's last third; blocked, it goes round. Touchdown when the feet reach the ground under them; the body eases to the slope's pitch, speed falls to zero over the absorb, the wings fold. |
| 10 | Tails | In the air and while landing, tail springs keep clear of the floor (as tentacles do), the flare pitch is at most 20°, and the tail lifts with it. |
| 11 | Level of detail | Distant flyers keep stepping the flight state (cheap: no posing) and play their air gait's baked cycle, tilted by bank and pitch. Takeoff and landing finish at full detail. Back at full detail flight resumes in place. |
| 12 | Actions | Actions run in the air with their goals adjusted: wings stay spread, `stop` holds slow flight or a hover. Leaping actions (with `hooks.leap`) are refused in the air. `fly()` and `land()` wait for a running action to end. |
| 13 | Water | The floor is the higher of ground and surface. A flyer enters the water medium only on touching the surface (a swimmer landing on water). `fly()` from water takes off from the surface; `land()` over water lands on it if it swims, else continues to ground. |
| 14 | Insects | `membrane.insect` styles its stroke by shape: broad (moths, butterflies) a large, near-vertical stroke (plane 20°, amplitude 65°, twist 25°) and `glide`; round or narrow a flatter one (plane 55°, amplitude 55°, twist 40°). A hind wing follows its same-side forewing (same stroke and twist, a 0.05 phase lag), so they never cross. A case lifts 35° and opens forward before the wings it covers spread. |

## The state machine

**Takeoff** (`fly()`, or a height out of reach): it turns to face where it goes and crouches
until the wings are spread (0.25 s × timeScale at least), then leaps straight up with
`v_y = √(2g · 1.5 · hip)` (10.2's ballistics, its own arc, no action needed), stroking at 1.3 ×
`f` and full amplitude; the `takeoff` event fires and `medium` becomes `air`. At the top of the
arc powered flight begins: it accelerates at 0.35 g toward slow-flight speed while climbing at
20°, then toward its cruise. A legless winged body skips the crouch.

**Flight**, each step:
1. **Target**: the `moveTo` point, or the asked height above the floor (`fly({ height })`,
   default decision 7). With no target it loiters: hovers (if it can) or circles its takeoff point.
2. **Speed** toward cruise (or the asked speed), never under `v_min` unless it hovers; near the
   target it slows. Hoverers stop and hover.
3. **Heading**: turn rate up to `g tan(bank) / max(v, v_min)`, capped at 1.5 rad/s; bank from the
   gait's `bank` (default 40°, at most 70°) and clamped by tip clearance (decision 7).
4. **Height**: climb up to `v sin 15°` flapping (or 0.5 √(g·hip) hovering), descend up to
   `v sin 25°`; the floor is never closer than tip clearance allows.
5. **Gait**: `hover` below 0.3 × cruise (leaving above 0.45), `glide` by the height band
   (decision 8), else `flap`; changes blend stroke settings over 0.3 s, and a glide begins at the
   top of a stroke.
6. **Wings** stroke (decision 6) with phase advancing at `f` (× `rate`, × 1.3 climbing, × 0.8
   descending); one `flap` event per beat at the downstroke's start.
7. **Body**: pitch with the flight path (35° nose-up hovering), roll with the bank, both pivoting
   about the wing roots' centroid so the wings stay put. Legs tuck in the body's own frame:
   forelegs folded back under the chest, hind legs trailing. Tail springs trail and keep clear.
   Walking motion keyed to the gait phase (sway, arm swing, sprawl wave, flex) runs only on land.

**Landing** (`land(target?)`; with none, the first clear ground ahead at its approach distance;
over water a swimmer lands on the surface): plan, fly the approach at `v_min`, flare, touch down
as decision 9, then `land` fires, `medium` becomes `land` (or `water`) and it walks (or swims).
Hoverers descend vertically onto the point instead. A legless flyer touches down on its lowest
body point and lays its slither trail along its posed spine.

## API

- `fly(options?: { height?, speed? })`, `land(target?)`, `flying` (true from the takeoff crouch
  to touchdown). `moveTo({ x, y?, z })`: in the air `y` is a world height; without `y` it holds
  its height above the floor. `stop()` in the air hovers or loiters; `drive(speed, heading)`
  flies straight at that speed (at least `v_min`), holding height; `lockGait` takes air gaits.
- `place(x, z, heading, ground, water, { y?, flying? })` and `spawn({ flying, height })` start a
  creature in the air at cruise.
- Events: `takeoff` and `land` (no `action` field; 10.2's leaps keep theirs), `flap` (one per
  beat), `medium` (`air` at liftoff, `land`/`water` at touchdown), `gait`.
- Scenarios: grounds `"flat"`, `"course"` or `{ "slope": degrees, "toward": degrees }`; calls
  `fly` (`height`, `speed`) and `land` (`to`); `moveTo` and `follow` take `[x, y, z]`;
  `duration` up to 120 s. Filmstrips frame the spread wings and their stroke while it flies.

## Wing data compiled into the rig

`WingRig` gains:
- `lift: boolean` (false for cases and fins);
- `stroke: { axes: { out, lead, normal } (body bind frame), sign, amplitude, flex, twist,
  plane, lag, held? }` from the style (`hooks.wing`'s new `stroke` field, defaults the bat's:
  amplitude 50, flex 0.4, twist 10, plane 0) and the pairing of hind to fore wings;
- poses `flexed` (elbow, wrist and digits turned in the wing plane by the style's flex angles,
  feathers fanned closed with them) and `flight` for held wings (cases lifted).

Wing helper bones are recomputed after the strokes, so elbows and wrists stay smooth.

## Checks

`analyze` adds, for creatures with an air gait:
- `cannot_fly` (decision 5).
- A **flight run**, 30 s at most: take off from flat ground, fly a circle of 1.5 × its minimum
  turn radius (`v_min² / (g tan 60°)`) at the default height, descend gliding (flapping when it
  cannot glide), land on a 15° slope. It reports `flight`: `altitude` (worst deviation over
  level flight, as a share of the asked height), `wings` (wing points against the body, legs,
  arms, the other wings and the floor, strokes included), `landing` (the deepest any body, tail
  or foot point goes into the slope) and `touchdown` (vertical and horizontal speed). Warnings:
  `wing_intersection` ("while flying"), `hard_landing`.
- `speed.fly` (cruise) and `speed.slow`; descriptions say "It flies at about 24 m/s"; `rpg`
  gains `fly`.

Cadence, per-gait runs and walking checks consider land gaits only, and the walking runs stay
exactly as today for winged creatures.

## Clips and the runtime

- `fly`, `glide` and `hover` clips are baked analytically over whole beats (at least 0.5 s),
  frame by frame at exact phases, in level flight at cruise (hover at 0), the root at the origin.
  `takeoff` and `land` are baked with root motion, as 10.2's leaps. Air gaits never bake on land.
- The runtime's `Creature` gains `fly()`, `land()`, `flying`, `moveTo` with `y` and the spawn
  options; level of detail per decision 11.

## Budgets

Flight adds a point-mass step, a stroke turn per wing bone, the flexed blend and the stations;
ground samples are cached and spring floors skipped high above the floor. Motion ≤ 0.15 ms per
creature flying; walking unchanged.

## Done when

The dragon, bat, wyvern and moth examples fly a scripted course (take off, circle, glide, land
on a 15° slope) with no `wing_intersection` in flight, level-flight altitude within 10% of the
asked height, landings without penetration beyond 2% of hip height, filmstrips in the PR (also
the griffin's), and flying motion ≤ 0.15 ms per creature. `flight.test.ts` checks each of these,
uphill, downhill and across-slope landings, `cannot_fly`, the API and events, level-of-detail
round trips while flying, baked cycles, and that walking winged creatures are unchanged.

## Changes while building

- Gait modules declare `air: 'flapping' | 'gliding' | 'hovering'`, not `flap`, `glide` and
  `hover`: two of those are gait ids, and the core may not name a module.
- The upstroke flex turned the right wing forward instead of back; it takes the side's sign now.
  Fingers that start at the wrist hang from the forearm, so they swung with the elbow and not the
  wrist and swept into the body; they turn with the hand now.
- The flare beat at 1.2 times the amplitude and the wings struck the slope (and a griffin's
  feathers its back): it beats at the normal amplitude, and over its second half the beat dies
  and the wings rise 30°, as a bird's do as its feet reach down.
- Hoverers flared like everything else and touched down at speed; they slow to a hover over
  the point and come straight down (`flightStage` `descend`).
- On a slope the body met the ground level and its feet went into it: it pitches to the slope
  as its feet reach down, pivoting about its middle rather than its wing roots, and reaching feet
  stop on the ground.
- The landing's absorb lowered the body and never let it up; it is a 0.4 s dip now. Tails keep
  clear of the ground through it, by the whole of their radius, and follow the slope between
  ground samples.
- The strokes stopped at touchdown and the wings jumped to the spread pose; they die away over
  0.3 s, never below it.
- `analyze` circles at slow-flight speed (a circle at a dragon's cruise would take 22 s) and checks
  wings on one side against each other; wings on opposite sides may meet over the back, as
  insects' do.
- The griffin's wings carried 910 N/m²: they are longer (1.45) with longer feathers (1.15), at
  620. The rhino beetle's carried 1,856, and hind wings big enough to lift it hang below its body
  when folded, so it is grounded (`"media": { "air": false }`), as a beetle that heavy would be.
- `update(dt, { pose: false })` steps a flyer without posing, for level of detail; the next
  posed update tucks the legs and settles the springs where the body is.
