# Design: the gallery (milestone 12.2)

A static site of curated creatures: the examples, every theme across seeds, and the creatures
agents wrote for the eval prompts, each with a render, a live viewer, its blueprint and a `.glb`
to download, and a link that opens it in the sandbox. Plan 2 rates it medium (lowered): it
assembles finished pieces. CI builds it as an artifact; publishing it waits for the owner (plan
2's open question 3, where it should live).

## Decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What it shows | Three groups. The 32 examples, each beside its committed contact sheet. Every theme at seeds 1 to 6 (42), as `spawnforge generate --theme <t> --seed <n>` makes them. The 40 creatures agents wrote for gate 9's suites A and B, the last attempt of each prompt, with the prompt it answers. |
| 2 | Theme creatures | Generating takes a sixth of a second each, seven seconds for the page: `pnpm generate` writes them to `apps/gallery/src/themes.json` (`scripts/gallery-themes.ts`), so CI fails when they go stale, like the catalogue. |
| 3 | Thumbnails | Rendered in the page, not committed: one off-screen renderer draws each card's creature from three-quarters (compiled in a worker at low quality) when the card scrolls into view, one at a time. Nothing to re-render when the pipeline changes, and no 80 more images in the repository. The examples keep their committed sheets on their pages. |
| 4 | Pages | Hash routes (`#/`, `#/theme`, `#/example/grey-wolf`) and relative URLs (`base: './'`), so the built site works from any folder on any static host. |
| 5 | The viewer | The creature at medium quality, idling on a floor (or swimming in place in open water if it only swims), turning slowly; drag to orbit, a button per action. `?webgl` forces the WebGL 2 backend. |
| 6 | Downloads | The blueprint as JSON, and a `.glb` exported in the page as `spawnforge export` writes it: `exportGlb` moved from the sandbox into `@spawnforge/three/glb` (an entry point of its own, so games that never export load no bake), with the texture bake on workers and PNG maps turning JPEG past 8 MB. |
| 7 | Open in sandbox | A link to the sandbox with the blueprint in its hash (`#blueprint=` and base64url JSON) and its name in `?creature`; the sandbox reads it. The sandbox's address is `VITE_SANDBOX_URL` at build time, the dev server's by default. |
| 8 | The test | `apps/gallery/src/gallery.test.ts` builds the site, serves the build, and opens it in headless Chromium: every card in its group (32, 42 and 40) and thumbnails rendering without errors; a group's page; an example's page with its viewer drawing, its sheet loaded, a blueprint download that validates, a sandbox link carrying the same blueprint and a `.glb` download with the blueprint in its extras; a theme's and an agent's creature opening. CI uploads the build as an artifact. |

## Done when

The site builds and passes a headless smoke test of its pages.
