# Design: feet, hands and stance (milestone 8.2)

Plan 1 has one foot, `foot.claw`: short toes fanned on the ground from the leg's tip, or fingers
continuing an arm. 8.2 builds the four feet and the hand that 7.3 stubbed, and gives
`limbs[].stance` a pose and a motion. Everything goes through the foot slot's hooks; the core
names no foot.

## Today

- A leg's tip (the ankle) is solved to `footH = max(tip radius, 0.012 L)` above the ground, and
  the posture fit lifts the body to match (`skeleton.ts`, `legPlan`).
- The foot's `toes` hook grows toe chains from the ankle; each pair of points becomes a bone
  parented to the last leg bone, skinned like the rest (in the field, or as a swept tube when
  thin). Its `build` hook gets the toes as sockets and emits claws.
- In motion the ankle is the planted point; toes are aimed at their rest direction, turned with
  the body, every frame. Every foot-slide check measures the ankle.
- Nothing reads `stance`. `analyze` sizes feet from a `toeLength` parameter and stats count claws
  from `toes` and `clawLength`, by name.

## Stance

`stance` is the leg's, or its foot module's `stance`, or none.

- **None** (every plan 1 creature, `foot.claw`): today's pose and motion, exactly. Goldens and
  the motion tests of the existing examples do not move.
- **The ankle's height** comes from the foot: a module's new `footHeight(ctx, params)` hook
  returns it in metres (a hoof's height, a paw's thickness), given the tip radius, the scale,
  the role and the stance. Without the hook, the stance gives it: plantigrade keeps `footH`,
  digitigrade lifts the ankle to `max(footH, 2.2 × tip radius)` so the toes slope down to the
  ground, unguligrade to `max(footH, 3 × tip radius)`. `legPlan` uses it for both the posture fit
  and the IK target, so the body rises with the foot and the leg keeps its shape.
- **The toes hook** gets `stance` and `footHeight` in its context, so a module shapes toes for it.

## Rolling feet

Legs with a stance and toes roll while planted:

- **Heel off**: in the last 35% of the stance, the planted ankle rises by
  `smoothstep × lift`, where `lift = toe length × sin(25°)` (plantigrade) or `× sin(18°)`
  (digitigrade); the leg's IK follows. The ankle moves only vertically, so it does not slide.
- **The toes stay down**: from landing, each toe's tip is a planted point. While the heel is up,
  a toe's first two bones are solved as two-bone IK from the ankle to that point, bending up at
  their joint like a ball of the foot; further bones keep their rest direction.
- **Toe off**: the toes keep aiming at their planted tips for the first 25% of the swing (until
  out of reach), then blend back to their rest direction.
- Unguligrade feet (a hoof on a vertical toe) do not roll: the hoof stays flat until lift-off.

The ankle is still the planted point for slide checks; it never moves horizontally while
planted, so foot slide is unchanged. The uneven-ground test, which holds a planted ankle within
3 cm of the ground, applies before heel-off.

## Modules

Sizes are in the tip radius `r` (metres) or in torso lengths `L`, as each parameter says. These
are as built, after renders of the new examples.

| Module | Stance | Ankle height | Toes | Built |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `foot.paw` | digitigrade | 1.5 r × size | 3–5 short thick digits fanned 18° apart, sloping from the ankle to knuckles on the ground 1.7 r ahead | a pad under each toe and a palm pad under the ankle (`padColor`), which lifts with the heel; claws hidden, short or long and curved |
| `foot.hoof` | unguligrade | (0.55 + 1.05 × height) r × size | one exactly upright toe, a slim pastern (two side by side when cloven) | a hoof wall (a lathe, about twice the pastern's radius, wider at the sole, its front sloped back) around each toe's lower two thirds, in `color` |
| `foot.talon` | digitigrade | 1.3 r | three long toes forward (±28°) and one back and inward, 3 bones each, curled by `grip` | a scaly ringed sheath over each toe bone (`color`) and a hooked talon at each tip (`talonColor`) |
| `foot.pad` | plantigrade | 1.15 r × width | one broad upright toe, `width × r` wide, its rounded end on the ground | blunt nails around the front of the sole (`nailColor`) |
| `hand.grasp` | — | (none: plan 1's) | on arms, fingers side by side across the palm curling forward, and a thumb from the inner side; on legs, long toes laid forward and a big toe turned inward | nails, or claws when `claws` > 0 |

- A new hook `claws(params)` returns `{ count, length }` (length in torso lengths) for stats;
  `foot.claw` implements it too, so stats stop reading parameter names. `analyze` sizes a foot
  from its compiled toes (the farthest toe tip from the ankle), not from `toeLength`.
- `foot.hoof` and `foot.pad` are made of one vertical toe each so the motion keeps them upright
  and flat while the leg above them swings.
- Foot parts place pads, nails and sheaths with two additions to the build context: each toe's
  joints, bones and the limb bone it grows from, and `frame()`, a socket at any point bound to a
  bone.
- Each foot is still one module file; their shared geometry helpers live in `parts/_feet.ts`,
  which the pack index skips.

## Examples and checks

- New examples: a grey wolf with paws, a boar with cloven hooves, a raptor with talons; the bog
  troll gets `hand.grasp` hands.
- Tests: each module through the harness (on a leg, and `hand.grasp` on an arm too), with its
  enum and boolean options; stance heights per stance and module; a rolling foot keeps its toe
  tips planted and its ankle still horizontally through a walk; no stance changes nothing
  (goldens of the existing examples unchanged).
- The motion tests and `analyze` on the new examples: no foot slide over plan 1's bars.
